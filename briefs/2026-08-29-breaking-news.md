# Breaking news that matters for trading — week ending Fri 28 Aug 2026

Compiled 2026-08-29 (Saturday, markets closed). Ranked by breadth of impact:
how many books a desk has to re-mark because of it.

## 1. Warsh turns hawkish at his first Jackson Hole — Fri 28 Aug

Fed Chair **Kevin Warsh** said the central bank will "have work to do" if
policymakers don't get confidence inflation is heading to 2% — the closest he
has come to acknowledging hikes may be needed. BMO's Vail Hartman called it
"a deliberately hawkish speech that will put to rest any concerns about the
Fed's willingness to raise rates."

| Instrument | Move |
|---|---|
| 2y UST | **+11bp to ~4.34–4.35%** — highest in a month |
| 10y UST | +5bp to 4.72% |
| 30y UST | +1.6bp to 5.206% |
| Equities | Reversed opening gains, closed lower |

Rate-hike odds for September repriced hard, though sources disagree on the
level: CME FedWatch was cited at **45.7% (from 35.4%)**, other desks at
**60% (from 35%)**. Either way it's a bear flattener — the front end did the
work, the long end barely moved.

**Why it's the top item:** it repriced the discount rate for every asset in
one speech. Everything below is downstream of this.

## 2. Nvidia: beat and raise, but the margin line broke — reported Wed 26 Aug AMC

- Revenue **$96.2B** vs $92.2B expected, more than doubling YoY
- Data center **$89B, +117%** vs ~$85B expected
- Adj. EPS **$2.22** vs $2.10
- Q3 guide **$108B** vs $104.2B expected — *assuming zero China revenue*
- **The problem:** gross margin guided to trough at **71–72% by fiscal Q4,
  down from 75%**, on rising memory costs

**Reaction, and a caveat:** the immediate print was negative — shares fell
1.8% after hours having closed the regular session 1.6% lower. Reporting then
has NVDA **+8.7%** in the following session, with the S&P +0.7% to 7,730.99
and the Nasdaq +1.6% to 26,541.35. Sources also disagree on whether the report
landed Aug 26 or Aug 27. Treat the reaction path as: *sold the margin line
first, bought the revenue guide second.*

**Tradeable read:** the AI trade is no longer a demand story, it's a **cost of
memory** story. That's a supply-chain input, and it re-rates the whole
semi/hyperscaler complex, not just NVDA.

## 3. Black Sea grain corridor shut — running through August

Reciprocal Russian/Ukrainian strikes on ports have taken Black Sea grain
shipments to a near-standstill, at peak harvest.

- **>97% of grain export capacity lost** versus last season
- Ukraine exports **−75%** in the first half of August YoY; currently no
  shipments from Ukraine's Black Sea terminals
- Russia's August wheat exports ~**1.8Mt — lowest for the month since 2010**
- The region historically carried ~70% of Russian grain exports

| Contract | Week | Month | YTD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat | **+10%** | +20% | **+51%** |
| Corn | +6% | +16% | +22% |
| Soybeans | +3% | +7% | +22% |

**Tradeable read:** this is a **physical** supply shock with a storage
overhang behind it — Russia harvested ~140Mt with nowhere to ship it. The
inventory is real and it comes back the moment the corridor reopens. Long ags
here is long a shipping-lane headline, not long a crop failure.

## 4. Trump moves on meat processing — Fri 28 Aug

Trump posted on Truth Social that he is "authorizing legal documents to be
drawn" letting farmers and ranchers process their own food, calling the
dominant processors "a nasty monopoly." **Cargill, Tyson, JBS USA and National
Beef control ~85% of US meat processing.**

- **TSN and JBS fell >2.5% premarket**
- Ag Secretary Brooke Rollins previewed follow-ups for the coming week:
  interstate meat sales, repealing old guidance, funding for small processors,
  new labelling standards

**Tradeable read:** no legal text exists yet — this is a Truth Social post,
not a rule. But the follow-up calendar means headline risk on the protein
names is live all next week.

## 5. Cross-asset: crude unwound the war premium, positioning is the tripwire

- **WTI −4.82% to $81.01**, **Brent −5.57% to $85.32** — third straight
  session lower, on easing geopolitical tension and softer sanctions impact
- **Bitcoin +22% on the week**, through its 200dma near $69,000, peaking
  $79,200 and settling ~$77,025; a Treasury long-bond buyback triggered a ~$4B
  short squeeze, US spot ETFs took ~$650M net
- **CTAs are underweight equities** after the Iran conflict left them
  positioned defensively. A **3% S&P drawdown could trigger $100B+ of
  systematic selling** — that tripwire is live

*Lower confidence on this item: the underlying source is a monthly round-up
and its dating within August is imprecise. Verify the levels before sizing.*

**Tradeable read:** hawkish Fed (#1) plus a live CTA tripwire is the
combination that turns an orderly 2% pullback into a disorderly one.

## What connects them

Two shocks are **supply-side and inflationary** — grain (#3) and memory costs
inside Nvidia's margin guide (#2) — landing in the same week the Fed chair
said he'd hike if inflation doesn't cooperate (#1). That is the setup worth
watching: goods-price pressure re-emerging into a Fed that has just told you
its reaction function.
